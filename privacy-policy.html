Privacy Policy

Trump Truth Social Alert — Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

Trump Truth Social Alert is a browser extension that monitors President Trump's Truth Social account and delivers notifications. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Chrome Extension — Data Collection

The Chrome extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party services.

Chrome Extension — Network Requests

The extension makes periodic requests to our own relay server solely to check for new posts. No user data, identifiers, or authentication credentials are sent with these requests.

Chrome Extension — Local Storage

The extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

  • The ID of the most recently seen post (to detect new ones)
  • Your notification preferences (enabled/disabled, polling interval)
  • A cache of recent posts for display in the popup

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Chrome Extension — Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions needed to function: alarms (for periodic checking), notifications (to alert you), storage (to save preferences), offscreen (to keep the background worker awake between checks), and access to our relay server (to fetch posts).

Email Alerts (Ended)

Until November 20, 2026 we also ran an email alert service. It has ended, and we no longer collect email addresses.

  • Subscribers' email addresses and alert preferences were deleted from our database after the service ended.
  • Copies may remain for a limited time in our database provider's backups and in our email provider's delivery logs, which expire on those providers' normal schedules.
  • We never sold or rented subscriber email addresses. They were shared only with the providers that stored them and delivered the emails (Supabase and Resend).

Website — Analytics

The trumptruthalerts.com website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages they use. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects standard usage data, including your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP, the pages you view, and referring links.

This analytics data is not linked to you personally. We use analytics only in aggregate, to see what is working on the site.

If you would rather not be measured, you can block analytics cookies in your browser settings, use a content blocker, or install Google's official opt-out browser add-on. Blocking analytics does not affect the extension in any way.

The Chrome extension contains no analytics and is unaffected by any of the above.

Service Providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run the website and the extension's relay server. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job, and none of them are permitted to sell your data:

  • Supabase — hosts the database of Truth Social posts that the extension reads. It holds no personal data.
  • Railway — hosts the website and the API the extension reads from.
  • Google Analytics — website traffic measurement, as described above.
